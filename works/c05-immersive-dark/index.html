01 策展論述

The Unseen Narrative.

In a world saturated with visual noise, we seek clarity. aqualux 2026 presents "The Unseen Narrative," an exploration into design's fundamental truths. This exhibition strips away the superfluous, focusing on the core. It is an invitation to perceive.

Each artifact, each interaction, is rendered with deliberate precision. Surfaces recede into carefully tiered depths. Light is a purposeful accent, not a flamboyant display. We believe that true understanding emerges from the absence of distraction.

"Design, in its true tones."
— aqualux.dev

This is not merely a dark mode. It is an immersive environment. A space where negative space speaks volumes, and every element is given room to breathe. Discover the power of restraint. Experience the profound impact of the minimal. This is the spectrum of immersive dark.

04 五大展區

The Spectrum Zones.

Z01

主流頻譜

Mainstream Frequency

探索當代設計的主流脈絡，在極簡與功能之間尋找平衡。五件作品展示了如何在廣泛接受的框架內實現創新。

5F-A1 // 5 WORKS

Z02

時光殘響

Retro Echo

回溯設計的黃金年代，將經典元素以現代語彙重新詮釋。六件作品展現了時間與風格的對話。

5F-A2 // 6 WORKS

Z03

前衛實驗室

Avant Lab

挑戰傳統邊界，探索設計的未來可能性。六件作品透過實驗性的材料與形式，預示了未來的趨勢。

5F-B1 // 6 WORKS

Z04

文化光譜

Cultural Spectrum

從豐厚的文化底蘊中汲取靈感，將傳統美學融入當代設計。五件作品展現了跨文化的對話與創新。

5F-B2 // 5 WORKS

Z05

動態廳

Motion Hall

專注於動態設計與互動體驗，十五件作品透過光影、聲音與觸覺，創造沉浸式的感官旅程。

5F-C1 // 15 WORKS
