01 策展論述

THE MANIFESTO

WELCOME, FELLOW VISIONARIES! The AQUALUX 2026 Design Spectrum Exhibition proudly presents a deep dive into the unapologetic world of AMERICAN RETRO PRINT. This isn't your average, sterile gallery experience. We're tearing down the walls and splashing vibrant colors across the canvas of design history.

From the gritty halftone patterns of vintage comics to the bold, in-your-face typography of 70s concert posters, this movement is a celebration of raw, unfiltered creativity. We honor the imperfections, the misregistrations, the ink bleeds – because that’s where the true SOUL of design resides.

"THIS IS A CALL TO ARMS FOR THE BOLD, THE BRASH, THE UNCONVENTIONAL. FORGET YOUR MINIMALISM. EMBRACE THE NOISE. EMBRACE THE COLOR. EMBRACE THE POWER!"

Join us as we explore how contemporary designers are reinterpreting this powerful aesthetic, pushing boundaries, and reminding us that design can be both beautiful and rebellious. Prepare to be INSPIRED, to be CHALLENGED, and to see design through a new, vibrant lens.
