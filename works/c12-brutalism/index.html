CURATORIAL STATEMENT

キュレーション ステートメント

This exhibition is not a gentle whisper; it is a concrete declaration. We reject the veneer of superficial aesthetics, the seductive polish that obscures purpose. Here, form follows function with an uncompromising, almost aggressive honesty. This is the unadorned truth of design.

「建築的本質，在於其堅實的材料和結構。設計的本質，在於其無可妥協的功能性。我們將這些基礎元素，以最粗獷、最直接的方式呈現。」

-- C12 MANIFESTO

The BRUTALIST aesthetic, often misunderstood, is a testament to raw power and structural integrity. It embraces the inherent qualities of materials – the rough texture of concrete, the cold gleam of steel, the stark contrast of light and shadow. It is a dialogue between human intent and material reality, without intermediary.

In a world saturated with ephemeral trends, we present a counter-narrative: design that is permanent, uncompromising, and unapologetically real. This is not for the faint of heart; it is for those who seek authenticity in its most visceral form.
